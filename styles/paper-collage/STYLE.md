# Paper collage

Pieces of paper land on a cream sheet, cut against dark b-roll under a halftone screen. Works because every piece is
evidence: a real photo, a real headline, a number with its source.

## How each element is made

| Element | Recipe |
|---|---|
| Paper sheet | `#efe9dc` with a grain texture multiplied at ~0.5; a slow push 1.03-1.05x per scene |
| Photo sticker | licensed photo, background removed (BiRefNet), 10-14 px white border, soft drop shadow, tilt -4 to +3 deg |
| Clipping | cream card, torn bottom edge (SVG mask), small caps kicker line, Anton headline, Special Elite body |
| Stamp | red `#d8402c` box outline and text, multiplied at 0.92, lands with box stamp |
| Typed label | Special Elite on a cream strip, typed letter by letter |
| Key strip | black `#141311` bar, cream Anton text, slapped in |
| B-roll | any footage without people, desaturated 35 %, contrast 1.18, brightness 0.7, 8 px halftone, vignette |
| Captions | Inter, white on a dark box at the bottom, a phrase at a time |

The full build, with every helper, is the worked example: `films/explainer/paper-collage/project/`.

## Use it when

The story is true and has evidence to show: people, dates, documents, money. It reads as journalism.

## Do not use it when

There are no real photos or documents to cut out (it turns into empty paper), the subject is a product demo (screens
fight the paper), or the film needs to feel futuristic.
