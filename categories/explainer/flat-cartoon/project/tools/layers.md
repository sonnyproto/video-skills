# Scenery plates

Made with GPT Image 2.6 through Higgsfield: `aspect_ratio 16:9`, `quality high`, `resolution 2k`, `background transparent`,
two candidates per plate, the first candidate picked each time. Downscaled to 2304 px wide for the project.

## far.png

Background layer for a flat vector science-explainer animation (far distance layer). A long horizontal band of distant
soft rolling hills and gentle low mountains spanning the full width, occupying only the bottom 40% of the frame;
everything above the hill line is fully transparent (no sky, no sun, no clouds, no stars). Style: clean flat vector
illustration, bold but hazy colors, no outlines, smooth rounded organic shapes, cel-shaded with flat shadow shapes and a
thin warm peach rim light (#ffb07a) along the hill tops facing a low sunset light from the LEFT. Atmospheric perspective:
light hazy lavender and dusty violet (#8a4fa3, #9a62b5), low contrast, a few tiny distant rounded tree silhouettes on the
ridges. Crisp edges, no texture noise, no people, no animals, no text, no buildings.

## mid.png

Background layer for a flat vector science-explainer animation (middle distance layer). Rolling grassy hills spanning the
full width, occupying only the bottom 32% of the frame; everything above the hill line is fully transparent (no sky, no
sun, no clouds). Style: clean flat vector illustration, saturated colors, no outlines, smooth rounded organic shapes,
cel-shaded: each shape has a base color, a flat darker purple-blue shadow shape on the right side, a lighter highlight on
the left, and a thin warm orange rim light on edges facing a low sunset light from the LEFT. Colors: rich violets
(#5a2f8f, #45237a), plum shadows, small clusters of rounded stylized teal-green trees and bushes (#3f8f6f with #245a54
shadows), a few rounded rocks, a faint winding path. Crisp edges, no texture noise, no people, no animals, no text, no
buildings.

## near.png

Foreground layer for a flat vector science-explainer animation. A gently curved grassy hilltop ground strip spanning the
full width, occupying only the bottom 24% of the frame; everything above it is fully transparent. The ground surface is
fairly flat and open in the center and center-right (a character will stand there later, so keep that area clear of
objects). On the far right edge, one rounded stylized tree with a chunky trunk. Scattered small details: tufts of grass,
a few tiny round flowers in pink and gold, small pebbles. Style: clean flat vector illustration, saturated colors, no
outlines, smooth rounded shapes, cel-shaded with flat darker shadow shapes on the right side of objects and a thin warm
orange rim light on edges facing a low sunset light from the LEFT. Colors: deep purple ground (#33195f, #2a1450) with a
lighter violet top edge (#7a3f9e), teal-green tree (#3f8f6f, shadow #245a54). Crisp edges, no texture noise, no people,
no animals, no text.
