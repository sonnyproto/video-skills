# Reference

The look is after the family of flat-vector science channels: saturated colour on dark grounds, rim light and glow,
round friendly characters, a little grain. It copies none of them. The character (a mint-green blob), the palette
(violet, coral, teal) and every shot are original, generated from the prompts in `project/tools/footage.md`.

Why it is generated and not drawn in code: a code-drawn SVG version of the same look was tried first and rejected as
ugly. Flat vector needs an illustrator's drawing; image and video models supply that, and code does the timing,
captions and mix.
