# tilt-shift

A look, not a whole film. Everything needed to build it is in this folder:

- `style.json`: the card (palette, type, texture, light, consistency, motion, pace, banned, what it fits)
- `STYLE.md`: LemoLab's full recipe: materials, colour, type, motion, camera grammar, sound, native moves, pitfalls
- `stills/`: three frames from LemoLab's demo film "Toy Town Rush Hour"

## Sample

No video-skills film yet. The stills and the measured pace come from LemoLab's demo film
([watch](https://github.com/lemomo-ai/lemo-opuscar/releases/download/films/tilt-shift.mp4), CC BY 4.0). Its engine lives in [lemo-opuscar](https://github.com/lemomo-ai/lemo-opuscar/tree/9eb7739a0fca4c6990fe966cb8b87b1930f7193a/styles/tilt-shift/demo)
and is not copied here. Sound in that film: tuned percussion in a minimalist pulse; a tape-stop into silence when real time arrives.

## Using it

1. Settle the film's one idea and its structure first (another entry in `categories/` can supply the recipe).
2. Show the person `stills/1.jpg`-`3.jpg` next to two other looks and let them choose.
3. Build every element from `STYLE.md`. Where a recipe's own style differs, this look wins on look and the recipe wins
   on everything else. Real people, logos, product UI and claims stay real.
4. Credit LemoLab with a link to lemo-opuscar wherever the stills are shown.
